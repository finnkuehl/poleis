- **M0 – Project setup** (`v0.0.0`): Vite, TypeScript, PixiJS, Vitest, ESLint/Prettier, CI, Pages deploy.
- **M1 – Map** (`v0.1.0`): hex math, seeded map generator, camera (touch + mouse), tile selection with info panel, seed via URL.
- **M2 – Economy** (`v0.2.0`): project in English; starting city, 12 settlement buildings, terrain placement, roads (A*), connection check, workers, production, demolish, controls.
- **M3 – Population & city levels** (`v0.3.0`): residents eat, grow, starve and pay taxes; city center +1 bread; oil/wine supply streak; advance to Polis/Metropolis; vineyard, winery, marketplace (trading), temple; pause button; city panel; turn summary; test mode `?sandbox`.
- **Balance pass** (`v0.3.1`): resource buildings on their resource (woodcutter on forest, mine on mountains), border towers, houses hold 6, tap a tile → "Build here".
- **M4 – Territory & military** (`v0.4.0`): barracks and units, movement (terrain costs, roads, 1-tile rule, explored tiles only), combat with preview (walled city centers), upkeep/healing, capturing, settlers founding cities, fog of war, passive enemy in test mode; workers working/free, "📊 Overview" economy sheet, drag & drop for units. Founding/capturing verified by automated tests only.
- **Move orders** (`v0.4.1`): send a unit to a far explored tile by drag & drop (or tap + "Go here (N turns)"); it walks on by itself each turn along the cheapest path, shows a faint line with a flag (turn numbers when selected) and stops when it arrives, the way is blocked, a new enemy comes into sight or it is attacked; "Stop" in the unit panel, the "Units" button skips busy units.

- **M5 – First complete game** (`v0.5.0`): start menu (name, color, 1–3 rivals, map size, seed with live map preview, victory types), AI civilizations Athens/Sparta/Corinth (utility-based economy, exploring, defense, attacks after turn 15, new cities, wonder from turn 70, fog-fair), turn order with instant AI turns and a summary of what you saw, world wonder, scores sheet, victory/defeat (wonder, conquest, points after 120 turns, last city lost), autosave every turn + 3 save slots, fair start positions for 4 civilizations.

- **Low priority per good** (`v0.5.1`, user request): toggle in the 📊 Overview – buildings of that good get workers only after all others, so production slows instead of stopping; saves from v0.5.0 are converted automatically.

- **M7 graphics, first part** (`v0.5.2`, PR #10): style chosen with the user from three keyframes (V1 isometric diorama); painted bright Mediterranean landscape (beaches, turquoise coast, deep sea, pines and cypresses, snowy peaks, dirt roads, double borders, soft fog); 20 building and 8 unit miniatures with owner-color layers, generated by `tools/art/` (Python, standard library); flat goods icons; drawn art replaces the emoji in the UI; start menu, end screen and scores with the miniatures; dev gallery `/art-preview.html`; reveal/watch test options. **After the first playtest:** buildings stand directly on the map (no round plateaus) at one shared human scale; the game draws the ground under them (yards, paved plazas, forest clearings, quarry/mine aprons, fields that fill their tile and fade softly into the land); the fishery is one drawn scene in 6 direction versions that faces the real water; terrain without hex patchwork (soft washes, wavy organic edges with color bleed), curved coast with rocks, cliffs and sailboats. **After further playtests:** golden hill country and snowy mountain ranges that fill and join their tiles (terraces under buildings, mines at the foot of a peak, quarries cut into the slope, coast cliffs into the sea); dense forests of drawn tree sprites; painted meadow grass with flower drifts; shared town ground with lanes, paving and gardens; textured gravel roads and stone paving; fields that join only their own kind and lie on top of hills; richer unit figures on bronze tokens; units step to the front of building tiles.

- **M6 – Seafaring** (branch `m6-seafaring`, not merged yet): harbor on plains at the coast (Polis; connection point like a storehouse; drawn in 6 direction versions toward the water), transport ship (2 places) and trireme trained in the harbor; ships sail on coast and ocean with move orders; land units board an adjacent own transport and land on an adjacent shore (both end the move), passengers travel along, cannot act and are lost with a sunk ship; trireme fights ships and coast units, archers shoot at ships, melee units cannot; settlers reach islands this way. Ship panel lists passengers, boarding tiles show an anchor, own ships show a passenger badge. Saves upgrade to version 3. The AI builds no harbors or ships yet. Ocean graphics: depth gradient, painted swells and ripples, sun glints, layered foam, reefs; decorative boats removed.

## Known limitations

- The AI does not use ships yet and does not see enemy ships as a threat (by design for M6).

- Smoothness on a real phone with the new graphics is not measured yet (40×40 map: ~330k terrain vertices, ~4,700 tree sprites, culled per row; desktop runs at ~50 fps in watch mode).

- AI balance not yet tuned with real play: AIs may be strong for beginners (numbers in `src/data/ai.ts`). AI turns have no animation (M7).
- Test mode (`&sandbox`) keeps its passive enemy and does not autosave.
- A dev-only UI preview lives at `/ui-preview.html` (not part of the production build).
